Source path pattern: Samples/<piece>/<articulation>/<mic>/*_rr1.flac (one mid-high velocity layer, close + overhead mics mixed)

Changes made for the MIDI Field Guide (midi.community): microphone channels mixed down, trimmed (leading silence removed, cut to at most 2.5 s with a short fade-out), converted to mono, level-normalized, and MP3-encoded (96 kbps, 44.1 kHz).
