Source folder: Chordophones/Zithers/Harpsichord, Flemish/Sustains/High/
Note naming: the source names notes with C3 = middle C (MIDI 60), so e.g. a source file named C4 is MIDI 72 here (verified by pitch analysis).

Changes made for the MIDI Field Guide (midi.community): trimmed (leading silence removed), converted to mono, level-normalized, shortened to at most 2.6 s with a fade-out, and MP3-encoded (96 kbps, 44.1 kHz).
