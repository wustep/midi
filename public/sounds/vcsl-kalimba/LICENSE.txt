Source folder: Idiophones/Plucked Idiophones/Kalimba, Kenya/
Note naming: the source names notes with C3 = middle C (MIDI 60), so e.g. a source file named C4 is MIDI 72 here (verified by pitch analysis).

Changes made for the MIDI Field Guide (midi.community): trimmed (leading silence removed), converted to mono, level-normalized, retuned to equal temperament (the instrument has a traditional tuning; shifts of 59: +9 cents, 63: -10 cents, 68: -44 cents, 73: -20 cents, 78: -36 cents), shortened to at most 2.2 s with a fade-out, and MP3-encoded (96 kbps, 44.1 kHz).
