Source path pattern: Keys/Organ/Quiet/NT5_Man3Quiet_<N>_rr1.wav (manual) and Keys/Organ/Quiet/NT5_PedalQuiet_<N>_rr1.wav (pedal, 36.mp3 only)
Note naming: the source numbers the Quiet set's samples with an index N, not a note name: NT5_Man3Quiet_N is MIDI N - 86 and NT5_PedalQuiet_N is MIDI N - 28 here (C4 = 60), verified by pitch analysis including the harmonic series, so there is no octave ambiguity. The low C (36.mp3) comes from the pedal division because the lowest manual samples are very quiet.

Changes made for the MIDI Field Guide (midi.community): trimmed (leading silence removed), converted to mono, level-normalized, a loop region chosen in the sustain with a crossfade baked in so it repeats seamlessly (loop points in data/samples.json), cut shortly after the loop end with a short fade-out, and MP3-encoded (96 kbps, 44.1 kHz).
